Building a TA Function from Zero to Full Capacity

An 800-person growth-stage company had never had an internal recruiter. We designed and built the entire TA infrastructure from the ground up while simultaneously filling critical roles.

The Challenge
No internal recruiting function. No process. No assessment framework. Reliance on external recruiters and ad-hoc hiring. Inconsistent candidate experience. No data on hiring metrics.

Our Approach
We didn't just fill open roles—we architected the system. We designed process workflows, implemented ATS infrastructure, built employer branding strategy, created interview frameworks, developed assessment tools, and established compensation structures aligned with market data.

"We don't just fill roles. We build the machine that fills roles."

The Outcome
30+ positions filled while simultaneously building sustainable infrastructure. The team now owns their own talent acquisition process. Candidate experience improved. Hiring speed accelerated. Placement quality went up. Most importantly: the client can now hire without external support.
