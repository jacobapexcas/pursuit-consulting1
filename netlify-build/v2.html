What We Do

Four ways to work with us.

We fit around your stage, your team, and the hire that actually matters this quarter.

01 · Embedded

Embedded Recruiting Solutions

A dedicated recruiter inside your team, owning end-to-end hiring for a defined engagement. Ideal when hiring volume warrants a partner but not a full TA function.

  • Dedicated recruiter embedded with leadership
  • Sourcing, screening, interview calibration
  • Offer strategy and close
  • Process and tooling left behind
02 · Advisory

Talent Acquisition Advisory

Strategic consulting and infrastructure builds for companies designing or rebuilding their hiring engine. We assess the current state, design the system, build it, and leave behind infrastructure that compounds long after the engagement ends.

  • Hiring plans tied to the business plan
  • ATS selection, configuration, rollout
  • Interview frameworks and scorecards
  • Compensation benchmarking and offer strategy
03 · Search

Contingency & Retained Search

Direct hire placements and retained searches for companies that need exceptional sales, go-to-market, engineering, and product talent. Run as contingency when speed matters and retained when stakes do. We'll tell you which model fits the role before we start.

  • Senior leadership and critical first hires
  • Specialized GTM, sales, engineering
  • Multi-hire campaigns for growth builds
  • Pricing tied to what the role requires
04 · Augmentation

Staff Augmentation

A fractional recruiter plugged into your team for the duration of a hiring sprint. We scale up for the surge and step back when volume normalizes. No long-term contracts. No idle headcount.

  • Fractional engagement on your team
  • Hours scoped to your hiring volume
  • Surge support during peak periods
  • No long-term contract required
